# Start here

1. Put this folder on your computer. Rename it to your business if you like.
2. Open it in **Claude Code** (the Claude desktop app → Code, pick this folder).
3. Type: **set me up**

Claude checks your computer, asks you to talk for five minutes about your business, fills in your prices, and makes your **lead board**: one page with every lead, on your phone.

## Every day after that

- **A lead comes in** → paste it to Claude and say **lead**. Or tap **New lead** on your board from anywhere.
- **On the board** → tap a lead: call or text them, see the house, trace the concrete with your finger, and the price shows up. Pick an option, send the quote, mark it won, done, paid.
- **Leads fill in by themselves** → leave one Claude window open on your computer and type **watch** followed by your board link (Share → copy link). Every lead you add on your phone gets looked up in a minute or two.
- **At the computer** → say **sync**. Claude files everything you did into each customer's folder.
- **Sending a quote** → on the board, open the lead's Quote and tap **See the customer's quote**: the PDF and an email in your voice (how you talk lives in `me/voice.md`). **Finalize quote** sends both from your phone.

## Good to know

- You need Claude Code with a paid Claude plan. Claude installs one small free add-on for photos.
- Your prices and customers stay in this folder and your private board. Only a lead's address is sent out, to free map services, to measure it.
- Satellite numbers are rough. Your tape on site wins.
- The prices that come with this folder are samples. Claude won't quote a real customer with them.
