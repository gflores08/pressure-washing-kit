# Pressure washing office

A back office for a pressure washing business, run by Claude from your phone.

## Set it up

1. Open the **Claude** app → **Code** → start a new session.
2. Paste this and send it:

```
Set up my pressure washing office. Get the kit: git clone https://github.com/gflores08/pressure-washing-kit then go into that folder and follow its CLAUDE.md. I'm saying: set me up, and yes, schedule the lead check on my board 5 times a day.
```

3. Talk for five minutes about your business when Claude asks.

You get your **lead board**: one page on your phone with every lead, a satellite photo of each house, the property line, and the price that builds as you trace the concrete with your finger.

## Every day after that

- **New lead** → tap **New lead** on your board. Claude checks your board 5 times a day (7, 10, 1, 4, 7) and fills in the house. In a hurry? Say "sync" in your setup chat.
- **On the board** → call or text them, trace the concrete, pick an option, send the quote (a PDF and an email in your voice), mark it won, done, paid.
- **Anything else** → go back to your setup chat and say it: "lead" with a pasted lead, "quote for Dana", "how are my leads".

## Good to know

- Needs a paid Claude plan (the Code tab).
- Your prices and customers stay on your private board in your Claude account. Only a lead's address goes out, to free map services, to measure it.
- Satellite numbers are rough. Your tape on site wins.
